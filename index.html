📖 閱讀：Learning from Home or Anywhere

Chip: Hey, Dina. What are you typing?
嘿，Dina。你在打什麼？
Dina: It’s a paper for my biology course.
這是我生物課的報告。
Chip: You went back to school?
你回學校唸書了？
Dina: Actually, it’s an online course.
事實上，這是一門線上課程。
Chip: Online? Don’t you find it lonely not being able to meet classmates?
線上？不能和同學見面，你不覺得孤單嗎？
Dina: Yes, sometimes. But the school is abroad and I’m busy with my job, so it’s the best pick for me.
是的，有時候會。但學校在國外，而且我工作很忙，所以這對我來說是最好的選擇。
Chip: Oh, you’re a foreign student. Which school are you with?
喔，你是個外國學生。你就讀哪所學校？
Dina: MIT.
麻省理工學院。
Chip: Wow. That’s one of the best colleges in America! Tell me more about online education!
哇。那是美國最頂尖的大學之一！跟我多說說關於線上教育的事吧！
Dina: I can watch video lessons of other MIT courses for free.
我可以免費觀看其他麻省理工學院課程的教學影片。
Chip: That’s great! It sounds like you’re gaining a lot from this online course.
太棒了！聽起來你從這門線上課程獲益良多。
Dina: Yeah, I can study whenever I want. So it doesn’t matter if I’m feeling tired.
對啊，我想什麼時候讀就什麼時候讀。所以就算我感到疲倦也沒關係。
Dina: I can simply take a break to do something relaxing and continue later.
我大可休息一下做些放鬆的事，稍後再繼續。
Dina: I’ve already covered a lot of ground in biology and other subjects by taking online courses.
透過上線上課程，我已經在生物和其他科目上學到了很多。
Chip: I know what you mean. Often, you might be feeling lazy but you still have to make the effort to go to class.
我懂你的意思。通常，你可能會覺得懶惰，但你還是得費盡力氣去上課。
Chip: But with online learning, you can catch up anytime.
但是有了線上學習，你隨時都能補上進度。
Dina: Exactly.
沒錯。
Chip: You’ve changed my mind. Online learning sounds fantastic!
你改變了我的想法。線上學習聽起來棒極了！

✍️ 關鍵文法

1. 連綴動詞 sound 的用法

說明：sound 當作「聽起來」時是連綴動詞，後面可以直接接形容詞，或者用 sound like + 名詞/子句。

接形容詞：Online learning sounds fantastic! (聽起來棒極了！)

接 like + 子句：It sounds like you're gaining a lot. (聽起來你獲益良多。)

2. make the effort to + V

說明：表示「付出努力去(做某事)」。

例句：You still have to make the effort to go to class. (你還是得費力去上課。)
